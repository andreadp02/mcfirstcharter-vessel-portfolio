Aurelia II

A masterpiece of modern maritime engineering, blending the timeless elegance of traditional sailing with unparalleled luxury and performance.

The Spirit of the Sea

Designed for those who seek the profound tranquility of the open ocean without compromising on refined comfort. Aurelia II cuts through the water with effortless grace, offering a sailing experience that is both exhilarating and supremely stable.

The expansive teak decks invite you to bask in the sun or enjoy al fresco dining under the stars. Below deck, the interior is a sanctuary of light oak and premium textiles, meticulously crafted to provide a sense of expansive space and serene luxury.
